Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ALFIJEER, operated by KAUSANITECH, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We built ALFIJEER around real local commerce, so we only collect what we genuinely need to connect buyers with nearby verified shops.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account detailsName, phone number, WhatsApp number, email, password (stored securely, never in plain text)
Seller business detailsBusiness name, category, address, shop photo, product listings and prices
Location dataGPS coordinates you provide or that your device shares, used to sort shops by distance and to power directions
Usage dataPages visited, searches made, and basic device/browser information, used to keep the platform working reliably

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To show buyers verified shops near them, sorted by real distance.
  • To verify a seller's identity and business before their shop goes live, as described in our Seller Rules & Standards.
  • To let buyers contact a seller directly by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To provide in-app directions to a shop, which — where enabled — uses Google Maps to calculate a route from your location.
  • To respond to support requests and investigate reports of fraud or rule violations.

3. What Other Users Can See

A seller's business name, category, shop photo, location, product listings, and contact details are shown publicly to buyers — that's the point of a shop listing. A buyer's personal details are not shown to sellers beyond what is necessary to complete contact, such as when a buyer chooses to message a seller.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the platform, including cloud hosting/database providers and — for the directions feature — Google Maps. These providers only receive the minimum data needed to perform their function (for example, your coordinates and a destination, when you ask for directions) and are bound by their own privacy terms.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted authentication tokens and access controls, to protect your information. No online system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe and only retain what's necessary to run the platform.

6. Your Choices

  • You can update your account and shop details at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can decline to share your device location — some features, like distance sorting and directions, will simply be less precise.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated personal data by contacting us below.

7. Children's Privacy

ALFIJEER is intended for users who are able to enter into a binding contract under Nigerian law. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as ALFIJEER grows. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.
