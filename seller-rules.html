For Sellers

Seller Rules & Standards

To protect buyers and maintain the trust ALFIJEER is built on, all sellers must follow these rules at all times.

✅

Sell Only What You Actually Have

Do not list products you cannot supply. Misleading buyers about product availability is grounds for immediate suspension.

💰

Display Honest Prices

The price shown to a buyer must be the price they pay. No hidden charges. No price changes after a buyer contacts you.

📞

Respond to Buyers Promptly

Respond to buyer messages within 24 hours. Sellers with consistently poor response rates risk losing their shop listing.

🛡️

No Fraud or Deception

Any seller found collecting payment without delivering goods will be permanently removed and reported. We take fraud very seriously.

🖼️

Use Real Product Photos

Only upload photos of your actual products. Using stock images or photos from other sellers is not permitted.

🔢

Your Shop Number is Non-Transferable

Your shop number belongs to you and your registered business only. It cannot be sold, lent, or shared with another person or business.

⚠️

Consequences for Violations

Minor violations result in a warning. Serious violations result in immediate shop suspension. Fraud results in permanent removal and blacklisting.

By selling on ALFIJEER you agree to these rules. Questions? WhatsApp our team
